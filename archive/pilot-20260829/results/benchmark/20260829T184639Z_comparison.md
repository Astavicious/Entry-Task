# General vs Improved Prompt Comparison

Batch: `20260829T184639Z`

This experiment used the same three requirements and the same three models for
both prompt profiles. Each run was allowed at most three generation/verification
attempts. All raw model responses, extracted Dafny sources, and verifier outputs
were preserved.

## Prompt Profiles

- `general`: asks for a complete Dafny program or repair intended to pass
  verification, without detailed specification-preservation rules.
- `improved`: uses the supplied success criteria, explicitly requests meaningful
  Dafny specifications, forbids omitted/weakened requirements and proof
  shortcuts, and tells repairs to preserve or strengthen specifications.

## Manual Specification Review

The review used the complete scenario requirements in `AGENTS.md`, including
the exact increment/reset behavior and the exact source/destination balance
changes. This is a reviewer assessment, separate from Dafny's result.

- All eight verified improved-profile programs explicitly represent the required
  behavior. No `assume`, axioms, `ensures true`, or weakened repair specifications
  were found.
- The improved `gpt-5.6-luna` authorized-access program expresses the intended
  grant, revoke, and access rules, but it is invalid Dafny because the repair used
  the unsupported expression `set<string>{}`. It therefore cannot be counted as
  a verified-and-faithful success.
- Eight of the nine general-profile programs explicitly cover the complete
  requirement set.
- The general `gpt-5.6-luna` account-transfer program implements
  `source := old(source) - amount` and
  `destination := old(destination) + amount`, but omits both formulas from its
  postconditions. Dafny proves conservation and non-negativity, but the complete
  transfer behavior is not explicitly specified.
- The improved `gpt-5.4` account-transfer result has one benign warning:
  `destination != null` is redundant because `Account` is non-nullable by
  default. Removing that precondition would eliminate the warning without
  weakening the meaningful transfer specification.
- The general `gpt-5.6-luna` authorized-access result has one deprecated-style
  warning caused by a semicolon after a field declaration. Removing that
  semicolon would eliminate the warning without changing behavior.
- The other two improved warnings came from a failed intermediate bounded-counter
  attempt. Its final verified attempt is warning-free.

## Interpretation

The improved prompt made specification intent more explicit, especially for
account transfer, and it successfully prevented proof shortcuts in this batch.
It did not improve first-attempt verification: both profiles scored 6/9. It also
made this small sample slower and less reliable overall because one repair loop
became stuck on incorrect Dafny syntax.

The defensible conclusion is not that either prompt is universally better. In
this batch, the general prompt was better for verification efficiency and final
success, while the improved prompt was better at insisting on explicit semantic
contracts. More repetitions are needed before treating the differences as a
stable model-quality result.
